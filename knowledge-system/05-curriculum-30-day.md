# G. Learning Loop & 30-Day Curriculum (Phase 7)

## 1. The learning loop

```
LEARN (5-min lesson, 1 concept)
  → DRILL (micro-practice: rewrite 5 lines / 3-turn constrained roleplay)
    → ROLEPLAY (full scenario, AI prospect in character)
      → FEEDBACK (coach mode: scores + 1 biggest fix + stronger example)
        → RETRY (same scenario, same hidden info; goal: improve the 1 fix)
          → SCORE (delta vs. previous attempt shown)
            → REAL-WORLD CHALLENGE (do it once today with a real person)
              → REFLECTION (journal: what happened, what you'd change)
                → WEEKLY REVIEW (every 7th day) → REPEAT
```

**Where each step comes from:**
- *Learn → apply immediately*: Carnegie's "stop frequently and ask yourself how you can apply each suggestion" and "apply these principles at every opportunity" (HWF Nine Suggestions).
- *Retry with one focus*: Carnegie's habit of reviewing blunders weekly, and Voss's insistence that the tools feel awkward at first and must be practiced until they're habits (NSTD Ch.3 Key Lessons).
- *Replay and reflect*: Lowndes's "Instant Replay": re-listen to important calls to catch what you missed (HTTA-summary Pt7).
- *Weekly review + journal*: Carnegie's weekly self-check and his record of names, dates and results (HWF Nine Suggestions).
- *Gamification with accountability*: Carnegie's "make a lively game" of paying a friend for each slip. In the product this becomes streaks and an optional accountability buddy.
- *Preparation before real calls*: Voss's one sheet (NSTD Appendix).

**Retry rule:** the user retries until they improve the targeted dimension by ≥10 points or have done 3 attempts. The coach changes *one* thing at a time (Carnegie Part 4: make the fault seem easy to correct).

## 2. Drill types (constrained practice modes)
| Code | Drill | Rule enforced by engine |
|---|---|---|
| DR-MIRROR | Mirror-only | User may only mirror, label or stay silent (send "…"). Goal: get 3 new facts. |
| DR-LABEL | Label rewrite | Rewrite 5 prospect statements as labels. |
| DR-QUESTION | Question converter | Convert closed/"why" questions into What/How. |
| DR-NO | No-oriented rewrite | Rewrite asks so "no" moves you forward. |
| DR-AUDIT | Accusation audit | List 3–5 accusations for a scenario, then deliver them in a 3-turn mini roleplay. |
| DR-SUMMARY | That's-right hunt | 3 attempts to summarize the AI's situation until it says "that's right". |
| DR-REFUSE | Four no's | Refuse 4 escalating demands without saying "no". |
| DR-PITCH | 25-word value line | Benefit statement ≤25 words, scored for clarity and relevance. |
| DR-CONCEDE | Concession ladder | Plan and run a shrinking concession sequence. |

## 3. 30-day curriculum

Difficulty rises each week: **W1 Listening & Empathy (Stairway steps 1–2) → W2 Rapport & Discovery (step 3 + questioning) → W3 Value & Objections (step 4) → W4 Negotiation & Closing (step 5) → Days 29–30 capstone.**

| Day | Skill | 5-minute lesson (key points) | AI roleplay | Real-world challenge | Reflection question | Success metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | How the coach works; the 10 dimensions; the Stairway | **Assessment:** S01 at difficulty 2, no hints | Notice how often you interrupt in one conversation today | What surprised you about your baseline? | Baseline saved for all dimensions |
| 2 | LIS.ATTEND | The listener controls the conversation. Quiet your inner voice. Slow down. | DR-MIRROR on S02 | In one conversation, let the other person talk ≥60% of the time | What did you learn that you would have missed by talking? | Talk ratio ≤ 50% |
| 3 | LIS.MIRROR + PAUSE | Mirror 1–3 words, curious tone, then silence. Don't stack mirrors. | S02 (diff 2) | Use 2 mirrors with a colleague or friend | What happened right after you mirrored? | ≥2 mirrors that lead to elaboration |
| 4 | EMP.LABEL | "It seems/sounds/looks like…". Never "I hear…". Pause after. | DR-LABEL, then S04 (diff 2) | Label one emotion in a real conversation (e.g. a stressed coworker) | Did the label calm things down or open them up? | ≥2 accurate labels; Empathy ≥ 60 |
| 5 | LIS.SUMMARY | Summary = paraphrase + label. "That's right" vs "You're right". | DR-SUMMARY on S03 | Summarize someone's point until they say "exactly" | How many tries did it take? Why? | "That's right" earned in ≤2 attempts |
| 6 | EMP.AUDIT | Say the worst thing they think before they do. Calm and specific. | DR-AUDIT, then S16 (diff 2) | Open one awkward message with an accusation audit | How did they react to you naming the negative? | Audit before the bad news; Empathy ≥ 65 |
| 7 | **Weekly review** | Review your week-1 log. Biggest blunders. One habit to keep. | Retry your lowest-scoring W1 scenario | Teach someone one technique | Which technique already feels natural? Which still feels fake? | Score up by ≥10 on the retried scenario |
| 8 | COM.OPENING | No-oriented openers ("Bad time?"). 25-word benefit line. Never a bare job title. | DR-PITCH, then S01 (diff 2) | Introduce your work to someone with a benefit line | Did they ask a follow-up question? | Timing check in first 2 turns; Clarity ≥ 65 |
| 9 | RAP.INTEREST + APPRECIATE | Be interested, not interesting. Specific, sincere appreciation. No flattery. | S02 (diff 3) | Give one specific, factual compliment with a reason | Was it specific enough to be believable? | ≥2 follow-ups on volunteered details; 0 generic flattery |
| 10 | RAP.STYLE | Analyst / Accommodator / Assertive. What silence means to each. Golden rule → "treat them how *they* need". | S07 (Analyst) then S05 (Assertive), diff 2 | Identify the style of 3 people you talk to today | What's *your* style, and where does it hurt you? | Correct style-adapted moves in both |
| 11 | DIS.CALIBRATED | What/How questions. Avoid "why". Ask 2–3 related questions from different angles. | DR-QUESTION, then S03 (diff 2) | Replace every "why" with "what" for a whole day | Which question got the longest answer? | ≥60% open questions; Questions ≥ 60 |
| 12 | DIS.PAIN | Cost of inaction in *their* numbers. Loss weighs more than gain (honestly). | S03 (diff 3) | Ask someone "What does it cost you to leave it as is?" about any problem | Did quantifying change their urgency? | Cost of inaction quantified by the prospect |
| 13 | DIS.DECISION + BLACKSWAN | Behind-the-table players. Treat "irrational" as a clue. Constraints and hidden desires. | S01 (diff 3): find the daughter | Map the decision makers of one real deal or project | Who did you previously ignore? | Black Swan revealed |
| 14 | **Weekly review** | Review W2 log. One sheet introduced. | Full S03 at diff 3 with a one sheet | Write a one sheet for a real upcoming conversation | What part of your prep did you actually use? | Overall ≥ 60 on S03 |
| 15 | INF.VALUE | Their wants, not yours. ≥70% of value lines use their words. | S13 (diff 2) | Rewrite one of your pitches/emails in "you" terms | How many "we/our" sentences did you cut? | Value ≥ 65; no pitch before discovery |
| 16 | INF.OWNERSHIP + STORY | Let the idea be theirs. Offer options. Dramatize with one concrete story. | S13 (diff 3) | Ask "What would you want to see happen?" before recommending something | Did they reach your idea on their own? | Prospect proposes or co-designs the solution |
| 17 | OBJ.ACKNOWLEDGE + NOARGUE | Never "you're wrong". Label first, then a calibrated question. Contested issue, not a contest with the person. | S12 (diff 3) | Disagree with someone today without contradicting them directly | How did "I may be wrong, but…" land? | 0 contradictions; Objections ≥ 60 |
| 18 | OBJ.PRICE | Don't discount on reflex. Uncover the real concern. Trade, don't give. | S04 (diff 3) | Notice one moment you'd normally discount or over-explain, and pause instead | What was the real concern behind "expensive"? | 0 unprompted discounts; real concern found |
| 19 | OBJ.STALL | "I'll think about it" / "send info". No-oriented asks. Earn 2 questions first. | S07 then S06 (diff 3) | Respond to a "let me think" with a label + calibrated question | What was behind the stall? | Real reason surfaced; dated next step |
| 20 | DIF.ANGER | Let them vent (safety valve). Label. Own your share without "but". "What would make this right?" | S08 (diff 3) | Next time someone complains, say nothing until they finish | How long did it take them to calm down? | No interruption of first vent; Empathy ≥ 70 |
| 21 | **Weekly review** | Review W3 log. Your most common objection response, before vs after. | Retry lowest W3 scenario at +1 difficulty | Ask a colleague to "fine" you for every "you're wrong" this week (Carnegie's game) | What pattern shows up in your mistakes? | Retry score +10 |
| 22 | NEG.ANCHOR | Information decides who goes first. Ranges with the real target at the low end. Withstand the extreme anchor. | S09 (diff 3) | Ask someone for their number before giving yours | What did you learn from letting them anchor? | Didn't anchor blind; range used if pushed |
| 23 | NEG.REFUSE | "How am I supposed to do that?" Four ways to say no. "Fair" handling. | DR-REFUSE, then S15 (diff 3) | Decline one request gracefully without the word "no" | Did they offer an alternative themselves? | 0 free concessions across ≥3 asks |
| 24 | NEG.CONCEDE + NONCASH | Shrinking concessions. Trade every move. Non-monetary terms. No splitting the difference. | DR-CONCEDE, then S14 (diff 3) | List 5 non-cash things you could ask for in your business | What non-cash item had the most value for the least cost? | Shrinking concession pattern; ≥1 non-cash term |
| 25 | NEG.PUNCH + SELF.NONEEDY | Take a punch without anger. "No deal is better than a bad deal". Deadlines are mostly self-imposed. | S05 (diff 4) | Notice one self-imposed deadline this week and question it | Where do you feel needy in deals? | Confidence ≥ 70; no concession right after a punch |
| 26 | CLO.CHECK + ASK | No-oriented trial close. Ask clearly. "Yes is nothing without How." | S10 (diff 3) | Make one explicit ask today that you'd normally avoid | What stopped you asking before? | Explicit ask made; next step has owner + date + action |
| 27 | CLO.EXECUTE + FOLLOWUP | Rule of Three. Behind-the-table check. "Have you given up on…?" for silent prospects. | S11 (email + call) | Send one no-oriented re-engagement message to a silent contact | Did you get a reply? What kind? | Email ≤ 60 words; Rule of Three in the call |
| 28 | **Weekly review** | Review W4 log. Compare with Day 1 baseline. | Retry lowest W4 scenario | Debrief a real negotiation against your one sheet | What changed most since Day 1? | Retry +10 |
| 29 | **Capstone A** | Full call: open → discover → value → objections → negotiate → close | S09 or S05 at diff 4, industry of the user's choice | Use your one sheet in a real sales conversation | Which tier of the Stairway did you skip under pressure? | Overall ≥ 70 |
| 30 | **Capstone B + re-assessment** | Re-run the Day 1 assessment | S01 at diff 3 (harder than Day 1) | Write your personal playbook: 5 techniques you'll keep | What's your next 30-day focus? | Overall ≥ Day-1 score + 20 |

**Real-world challenge safety note:** challenges are low-stakes by design (coworkers, friends, everyday transactions) until Week 4. This follows Voss's advice to practice the tools in everyday interactions first (NSTD Ch.3 Key Lessons) before high-stakes deals.
